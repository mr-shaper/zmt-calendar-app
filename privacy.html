隐私政策

更新日期：2026-11-24

  • 本应用只由开发者本人使用，只访问开发者本人授权的 Google 日历。
  • 使用的权限仅为“查看和编辑日历活动”（calendar.events），用途仅限：在本人日历里创建、查找、核对“重新登录”提醒事件。
  • 不读取、不保存、不出售、不分享任何其他日历内容或个人数据；授权令牌只保存在本人电脑上、仅本人可读的文件里。
  • 不向任何第三方传输 Google 用户数据。
  • 随时可以在 Google 账户的“第三方访问”页面撤销授权。

Privacy Policy

  • This app is used only by its developer and accesses only the developer's own Google Calendar.
  • The only scope used is calendar.events, solely to create, look up and verify relogin reminder events in the owner's calendar.
  • No other calendar content or personal data is read, stored, sold or shared. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer in an owner-only file.
  • No Google user data is transferred to any third party.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account third-party access page.
